HEXCHESS

Tactical Pushing & Promotion Strategy

Blue always moves first. In Player vs. AI, you are randomly assigned Blue or Orange when the match begins. Moves are final (no Undo).

HexChess — Quick Rules

Eliminate all 4 of your opponent's Regular/Promoted Tiles to win!

1. Turn Structure (2 Steps)

  • Step 1 — Pusher Action (Optional): Place a Pusher from your bench onto any empty non-center hex, OR move your Pusher or either Neutral Gray Pusher 1 hex.
  • Step 2 — Regular Action (Mandatory): Place a Regular Tile from your bench onto an empty Outer Rim hex, OR move a Regular/Promoted Tile on the board.
  • Tip: Click any Regular Tile directly at the start of your turn to skip Step 1 automatically!

2. Board Zones

  • Center Hex (Yellow): Regular Tiles entering here (by move or push) immediately Promote! Pushers can never enter Center. Not a safe zone.
  • Safe Zone Ring (Cyan Shields): Pieces here cannot be captured by normal movement, but can be pushed off or crushed by Pushers!
  • Outer Rim (Sand): Bench Regular Tiles deploy onto empty Outer Rim hexes.

3. Piece Movement

  • Regular Tile: Moves 1 hex in any direction to an empty hex or captures an unprotected enemy Regular/Promoted tile.
  • Promoted Tile (Gold Ring): Slides any number of unobstructed hexes in a straight line (Queen-like along hex axes).
  • Pusher Tile (Gray): Moves 1 hex. Can push a contiguous straight line of Regular/Promoted tiles (friendly or enemy) 1 hex!

4. Pushing, Crushing & Victory

  • Edge Push-Off: Any Regular/Promoted tile pushed off the board edge spirals into darkness and is captured!
  • Pusher Crush: Pushers cannot be pushed. Moving a Pusher directly into an adjacent Pusher is illegal, but pushing a line of tiles into another Pusher crushes (captures) the end tile against that stationary Pusher!
  • Draws: Stalemate (no legal Regular Action) or 30 full turns without a placement or capture.
